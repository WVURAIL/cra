# NOTICE

`cra` (Classroom Radio Astronomy) is an archived repository. It holds teaching
materials produced by participants in **DSPIRA**, an NSF-funded Research
Experiences for Teachers programme at the Lane Department of Computer Science
and Electrical Engineering, West Virginia University, run with the Green Bank
Observatory. Principal investigators: Professors Natalia Schmid and Kevin
Bandura. Green Bank Observatory coordinator: Richard Prestage.

The maintained material is at <https://wvurail.org/dspira-lessons/>. See
[`README.md`](README.md) for the redirect table.

## Authorship

Commits run from October 2018 to August 2026. Of 114 commits, 104 are by John
Makous and 9 by Pranav Sanghavi; the archival commit is by Dylan Gormley.

Where the documents themselves record an author:

* **John Makous** — the AP Physics velocity-curve unit (`VelocityCurve_*`,
  `APPhysics_AstroLesson7_GalacticPlaneObservation2_2019.pdf`) and the 2019
  horn assembly guides
* **Kevin Bandura** — `DSPIRA_Horn_Assembly.pdf` (2018) and
  `Neutral_Hydrogen_amplifier_v3.pdf`, the 21 cm LNA schematic
* **Alexis Emch** — `wavelengths_radiation_EM_intro.pdf`

The remaining PDFs carry no author metadata. Attribution for those rests on the
commit history.
